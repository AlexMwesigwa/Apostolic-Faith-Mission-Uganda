Why We Give

Giving is an act of worship and obedience to God. Through your generous contributions, we are able to spread the Gospel, support our community, and maintain our ministries. Every gift, no matter the size, makes a difference in advancing God's kingdom.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." - 2 Corinthians 9:7
