Our Story

Apostolic Faith Mission Uganda has been serving the people of Uganda for over 50 years. Founded in 1970, our church has grown from a small congregation to a nationwide movement with churches across the country.

We are committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ, building strong Christian communities, and serving those in need throughout Uganda.
