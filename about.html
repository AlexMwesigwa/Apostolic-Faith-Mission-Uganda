About Us

Learn about our history, mission, and vision

Our Story

Apostolic Faith Mission Uganda has been serving the people of Uganda for over 50 years. Founded in 1970, our church has grown from a small congregation to a nationwide movement with churches across the country.

We are committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ, building strong Christian communities, and serving those in need throughout Uganda.

Our Mission

To spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ throughout Uganda and beyond, making disciples and building strong Christian communities that transform lives and society.

Our Vision

To be a leading church in Uganda that demonstrates God's love through worship, fellowship, discipleship, and service to all people.

Our Values

  • Biblical Truth
  • Love and Compassion
  • Unity in Diversity
  • Service to Others
  • Integrity and Excellence
